Checkers & Draughts Theory

Mathematical Foundations, Retrograde Analysis, and Solved State Spaces

About This Publication

Welcome to the Checkers & Draughts Theory technical repository. This publication explores the rigorous mathematics of zero-sum combinatorial games, focusing on 8x8 American Checkers (English Draughts) and 10x10 International Draughts.

Key areas of exploration include Jonathan Schaeffer's 18-year computational proof of Chinook, endgame tablebase generation, bitboard state representation with fast bitwise parallel primitives, and modern neural network evaluation architectures.

Core Research Areas

  • Retrograde Analysis: Backward value propagation through 39 trillion states to resolve draw and win conditions.
  • Combinatorial Game Trees: Proof-number search algorithms and Alpha-Beta minimax cutoffs under mandatory capture constraints.
  • Board Geometry & Ergonomics: Asymmetry of single vs double corners and king mobility differences between Anglo-American and Continental rulesets.
